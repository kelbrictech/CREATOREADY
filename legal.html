What does the editor do?

CREATO’READY crops and resizes images and videos, and trims videos by start and end time. Its published source is available at GitHub. Your original source file remains unchanged.

Report a problem

To report a security concern, an accessibility issue, a suspected misuse of our site content, or a privacy question, email kelbrictech@gmail.com. Avoid attaching private media; a description of the issue is usually enough.
