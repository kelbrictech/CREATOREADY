CREATO’READY · POLICY & INFORMATION

Accessibility

We aim to make basic photo and video edits usable with readable text, visible controls and clear status messages.

Effective: 1 November 2026 · Last reviewed: 1 November 2026

Accessibility has not been independently audited or certified. Some interactive framing controls may be difficult to operate without pointing or touch input.

What we have included

Legal and help pages use semantic headings, visible keyboard focus and responsive typography. The editor uses labeled buttons, text alternatives where appropriate, accessible status messages and a guided workflow. Its first-use agreement screen offers keyboard-accessible policy links, an unchecked checkbox and a separate Continue action. The media-selection control supports Enter and Space after acceptance.

Known limitations

Moving the crop frame currently relies primarily on drag or touch gestures. Some browser-video playback controls are browser-dependent. FFmpeg processing can consume significant memory or processor time, and screen-reader announcements of progress may vary. We do not claim WCAG certification or full compatibility with every assistive technology.

Feedback and support

If you cannot complete a task, email kelbrictech@gmail.com. Describe your device, browser, assistive technology if relevant, and the step that failed. You need not attach the underlying private media. We will review practical improvements as the project evolves.

Alternatives

For tasks that cannot be completed accessibly with this editor, consider trusted browser, operating-system or desktop editing tools that offer keyboard-based cropping and timeline controls.
