CREATO’READY · POLICY & INFORMATION

Privacy Notice

Your selected videos and photos are processed in your browser. We do not run user accounts, advertising pixels or audience analytics in the application.

Effective: 1 November 2026 · Last reviewed: 1 November 2026

We do not upload your selected media files for editing. Websites and CDNs still receive standard requests to deliver the page and software assets; those requests can include technical information.

1. Who is responsible?

CREATO’READY is an independent project published through the GitHub account kelbrictech. For privacy and service inquiries, contact kelbrictech@gmail.com.

For data-protection questions, use the email above. The project is intended as a free public editing utility, primarily maintained from the Philippines.

2. Files you choose

Your browser reads your selected image or video locally. For video, it loads an FFmpeg WebAssembly engine in your browser, processes the file there, and prepares a download using a temporary browser object URL. The site does not send your original media, cropped frames, or exported files to our servers or a third-party processing API.

We have no account system, gallery, server-side upload endpoint or cloud backup for your media. We cannot view or recover your local files or exports.

3. Technical information transmitted

Loading a website necessarily sends network requests. Depending on where the site is hosted, the hosting provider (for example GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages) may process your IP address, browser or device information, timestamps, requested URLs and security-related connection data to deliver and protect the site. On first video export, the browser requests static FFmpeg engine files from jsDelivr, which may likewise receive normal request metadata. These software requests do not contain your selected media.

We do not intentionally use that technical information to create visitor profiles, analyze behavior or target advertising. The providers manage their own infrastructure logs under their respective policies, which may have independent retention schedules. Consult the applicable Cloudflare policy, GitHub privacy statement, and jsDelivr privacy information.

4. Cookies, caching and temporary data

The application does not deliberately create advertising, analytics or personalization cookies and does not use localStorage or IndexedDB to build a user profile. When you affirmatively accept Terms version TERMS-1.0, the editor writes a first-party sessionStorage item (key creatoready:accepted-terms, value TERMS-1.0) to remember acceptance for the current browser session. This item contains no personal identifier or timestamp and is not sent to the operator. It is normally cleared when the tab or session ends, although some browsers can restore sessions. If browser storage is disabled, acceptance is effective for the current page load only. A new Terms version requires renewed acceptance. Its service worker uses the browser's Cache Storage to save website assets and may cache downloaded video-engine components for future use. Media is handled through browser memory, temporary object URLs and files you choose to download; those downloads remain on your device until you delete them.

Hosting or security providers may use technically necessary security measures, including cookies in some configurations. See the Cookies & Local Storage notice for clearing instructions.

5. Email or support correspondence

If you voluntarily contact us, your email address and message are received by the mail provider and the project operator. We use that information only to respond to the inquiry, handle a legitimate request or keep a necessary record of the conversation. We retain it only as long as reasonably required for those purposes or applicable legal obligations, then delete it when no longer needed. Do not send private media unless absolutely necessary.

6. Purpose and legal basis

Technical web requests are used to deliver the site, prevent abuse and enable the editor to function. Where applicable privacy law requires a legal basis, service delivery and legitimate security interests may be relevant, subject to the governing legal conditions. Email communications are handled to answer requests you initiate. We do not rely on consent for advertising because no advertising or behavioral tracking is implemented.

7. Sharing and cross-border handling

We do not sell selected media, visitor profiles or contact lists. Hosting, CDN and email providers may process the limited technical or correspondence information involved in their services, potentially in other countries, according to their own systems and applicable protections.

8. Your choices and rights

You may decline the Terms without creating an account or selecting any media, avoid sending inquiries, clear sessionStorage and cached site data and delete your downloaded exports. Declining blocks editing, but the legal notices remain accessible. If you have sent us a message, contact kelbrictech@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion or other rights where applicable. We cannot retrieve media never uploaded to us. For third-party network logs, requests may need to be directed to the relevant provider.

9. Children, security and updates

The editor is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to collect personal details from children. Do not use it to create or distribute unlawful or exploitative content. We use HTTPS-supported static hosting and local processing where available; no browser or device can be guaranteed perfectly secure. We will revise this notice before materially changing how user media or personal information is handled.
