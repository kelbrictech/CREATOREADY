CREATO’READY · POLICY & INFORMATION

Cookies & Local Storage

No advertising cookies, audience analytics or account tracking are built into CREATO’READY.

Effective: 1 November 2026 · Last reviewed: 1 November 2026

A service worker may store the website's scripts, images and video-engine assets in your browser's cache. The editor does not store your selected videos or photos in a server database.

What the app stores locally

The site registers a service worker that may save a copy of static website files in the browser's Cache Storage. The browser may also cache files through its normal HTTP cache. This speeds up repeat visits and can allow parts of the editor to load offline. These stored files include the interface and FFmpeg assets; they do not include media you select or export.

Terms acceptance in sessionStorage

After you check the agreement box and select “Agree & Continue”, the editor stores one first-party sessionStorage item: creatoready:accepted-terms with value TERMS-1.0. It stores no email, name, account identifier, timestamp or server-side consent log. The item is generally temporary, although browser session restoration can preserve it. A material Terms update uses a new version and requires renewed acceptance. If sessionStorage is blocked, acceptance works only for the current loaded page.

This Terms acceptance is not consent for unrelated marketing or tracking.

Temporary media

While editing, the browser holds the selected media, previews and resulting output in memory. Browser object URLs are temporary references to those data. Downloaded files are saved only if you initiate a download; remove them through your device's file manager.

Cookies and analytics

The application's source does not set its own cookies or use analytics, advertising pixels, audience identifiers or cross-site tracking. Some web-hosting or security services may independently use necessary security cookies or collect connection information. We have not enabled a separate analytics implementation in the application code, and we do not promise that your browser or infrastructure provider makes no technical records. Learn more in our Privacy Notice.

How to clear stored site data

In Chrome or Edge, open the browser's site information or Settings → Privacy / Site settings → View permissions and data stored across sites. Find this website, then clear stored data for it, including the sessionStorage acceptance entry. You can also clear cached images/files in browser settings. Doing so may require downloading FFmpeg again before the next video export.

Consent banners

The Terms acceptance screen is not a marketing-cookie consent banner or blanket consent to personal-data processing. We do not show a marketing-cookie consent banner because the application does not ask users to accept marketing or analytics cookies. If the site's future configuration enables non-essential tracking or similar technologies, we must update this notice and implement any consent or choice mechanism required before enabling them.
